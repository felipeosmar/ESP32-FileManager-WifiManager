ℹ️ Information

NTP (Network Time Protocol) synchronizes your device's clock with internet time servers.

  • Ensure your device has internet connectivity
  • Choose a reliable NTP server (pool.ntp.org recommended)
  • Set the correct timezone offset for your region
  • Lower update intervals consume more network bandwidth
